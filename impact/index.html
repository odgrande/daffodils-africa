International Women's Day · Lagos, Nigeria · March 2024

IWD'24: Business Empowerment for Women

To celebrate International Women's Day 2024, Daffodils Africa successfully organised a social impact Ajo/Contribution to support two women-owned small-scale businesses in a low-income community in Lagos, Nigeria. The project was funded by contributions from different people committed to the goal. We helped two women striving to cater for their children through their businesses.

★ Our Impact in Action

Empowering Dreams

At Daffodils Africa, through collaborative efforts and unwavering commitment, we achieve remarkable outcomes, transformations, and uplifting achievements in low-income African communities.

Beneficiary Story · Lagos

Mrs Ade — Business Owner

Mrs Ade, a widow struggling to raise her children in difficult circumstances, received business empowerment support from Daffodils Africa. Thanks to the business support funds, she established her livelihood and gained the capacity to better provide for her children. Her contributions to her community continue to make a real difference.

Beneficiary Story · Lagos

Mrs Ogunsesin — Small Business Owner

Mrs Ogunsesin, a widow struggling to raise her children through a small business, received empowerment support through Daffodils Africa's IWD initiative. The support established her livelihood, empowering her to better care for her children's needs. Her story of resilience inspires communities around her.

Beneficiary Story · Children

Ezekiel and Samuel

Mrs Ogunsesin's children, Ezekiel and Samuel — they were in Primary 6. Whilst Samuel dreams of becoming a footballer when he grows up, Ezekiel wants to be intelligent and well-behaved. Daffodils Africa looks forward to giving them the tools to help them dream bigger and grow taller. Impact starts here.
