★ Our Story

From Compassion
to Coordinated Impact

We are a social enterprise with an unwavering commitment to implementing social impact projects for individuals, organisations, and government. At Daffodils Africa, we are not just about fancy reports and jargon. We are about real, compassionate people like you looking to do amazing yet simple steps to help real people thrive.

We recognise that progressive individuals and organisations desire to give back to society but may not understand the dynamics of systemic impact. Daffodils Africa is committed to helping you build lasting legacies — by weaving networks of support and helping you be part of a tribe. Because when people come together, amazing things happen.

★ What We Stand For

Our Core Values

♻️

Sustainability

We are committed to creating long-term, sustainable solutions that make a positive impact on the environment and society.

🤝

Inclusivity

We believe that everyone deserves an equal chance to thrive, regardless of their background, gender, or location.

🔍

Integrity

We do our work with transparency, accountability, and fairness. Every naira and every outcome is accounted for — always.

🎯

Impact

Daffodils Africa is committed to serving an impact in as many communities in Africa and across the world as possible.

★ The Founder

Meet the Founder

★ Founder & CEO

Ifeoluwa Oyebisi

Founder · Social Development Practitioner

An astute Social Development Practitioner with vast experience in the development sector. Driven by a burning passion for equal opportunities, she founded Daffodils Africa to empower every individual to deliver sustainable social impact.

She leaves no stone unturned in her quest for social justice. Seeing a glimmer of hope ignite in someone's eyes after connecting their resources to change another's world — that is what drives her. Not just the thrill of a successful project, but levelling the ground so that everyone gets a chance at opportunity.

"I'd like to level the ground so that everyone deserves a chance to simply have access to opportunities. Let's build a world where opportunity blooms for all."
